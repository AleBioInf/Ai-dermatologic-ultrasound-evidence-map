# Eligibility criteria

## Include

- Original human research.
- Dermatologic/cutaneous application.
- Ultrasound, HFUS, UHFUS, Doppler, or related diagnostic ultrasound data are an **actual model input**.
- A trained/evaluated AI, machine-learning, deep-learning, neural-network, or radiomics-ML model is used.
- Clinical or technical tasks can include diagnosis, classification, staging, segmentation, quantification, monitoring, or image-quality assessment.
- Full peer-reviewed journal article.

## Exclude

- Review, editorial, commentary, or non-empirical letter.
- Conference-only / abstract-only publication.
- Non-dermatologic domains (e.g. breast, thyroid, ophthalmic melanoma) without a dermatologic application.
- Ultrasound used only as a reference/comparator rather than as model input.
- Deterministic image processing or conventional statistical modelling without an explicit AI/ML framework.
- Animal, phantom, or ex-vivo-only studies.
- Therapeutic HIFU studies without diagnostic AI input.

## Scope stratification

The complete field includes clinical disease, technical/workflow, aesthetic, aging/physiology, and other cutaneous applications. For clinical-readiness analyses, a prespecified **core clinical** subset is used, restricted to disease-oriented diagnostic/staging studies.
