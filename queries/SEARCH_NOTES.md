# Search notes

**Search date:** 1 September 2026  
**Databases:** PubMed/MEDLINE and Scopus  
**Additional identification:** backward/forward citation chasing and dataset-lineage chasing.

The final PubMed strategy deliberately applies the non-dermatologic exclusions (`breast`, `ophthalmic`, etc.) to the **title only**, not title/abstract. This change was necessary because a title/abstract exclusion produced a false negative for Laverde-Saad et al. 2022, whose abstract mentions breast ultrasound as background.

No date, language, subject-area, or document-type filters were applied at the database-search stage. Eligibility restrictions were applied during screening.

## Important scope note

This repository reflects the **PubMed/MEDLINE + Scopus evidence map as of 1 September 2026**, supplemented by citation chasing. Embase and IEEE Xplore were not independently searched as separate databases in this version. Engineering literature indexed in Scopus and citation chasing was retained where eligible.
